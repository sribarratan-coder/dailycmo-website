One team that owns the whole funnel, not one slice of it.

You have probably hired an ads person before. Maybe a web designer too. Each did their part, and the leads still went quiet somewhere between the click and the sale. That gap is the job. Same room, same people. The team that plans your funnel is the team that builds it.

Sounds familiar?

The leads are there. They just never turn into anything.

You ran ads. People clicked. Then the enquiry sat in an inbox for three hours, someone replied "hi, how can I help", and that was the end of it.

You have a website, a Facebook page, a WhatsApp number and a Google Form somewhere. None of them talk to each other, so nobody can tell you how many leads came in last month, let alone where they came from.

Every time you get busy, marketing stops. Every time marketing stops, sales dips six weeks later. So you are either doing the marketing yourself at 11pm or paying three separate people who each blame the other two.

None of this is a traffic problem. It is a system problem, and systems can be built.

What you get

Built in this order, because each step feeds the next

This is the sequence every client engagement follows. Not tied to one industry. It depends entirely on what you sell and who buys it.

  1. Positioning and offer

    Who exactly you help, from what pain, to what outcome. If this is wrong, everything downstream is expensive. If it is right, marketing feels easy.

  2. Funnel pages for your main campaign

    Landing page, sales page, thank-you page. Written to be read to the end, tracked so you see where people drop off.

  3. Webinar funnels

    Registration, reminders, replay and follow-up. The format that has sold courses, cohorts and consultations for us and for clients since 2018.

  4. Automations

    Email and WhatsApp sequences that pick up where the page left off. Welcome, nurture, abandon-cart, reminders, booking confirmations. All on a CRM in your name.

  5. Ads

    Meta and Google, set up so every ringgit traces back to a lead. Creative, targeting and the boring weekly optimisation.

  6. Launch, track, reposition

    You get the numbers every week. When something is not converting, the offer gets fixed before the ad budget goes up.

Two ways to work

Whole funnel, or just the parts you are missing

Full build

You do not have a working funnel yet, or the one you have is held together with tape. Daily CMO builds pages, forms, automations and ads on a CRM set up under your name, then runs it with you.

Best for: founders launching a new offer, clinics and schools with enquiries but no system, anyone who has been "meaning to fix the website" for a year.

Plug into what you have

You already have a WordPress site and a team doing some of this. Daily CMO takes over the automations, emails and contact management, and connects them to your existing pages and ads.

Best for: businesses with a marketing person who needs the back half built, or an agency relationship that only covers ads.

Either way, everything lives in accounts you own. If you ever stop working with us, you keep the pages, the contacts, the automations and the ad accounts.

Questions

Things people ask before hiring us

How long does a full build take?

Pages and automations for one campaign usually go live within a few weeks of kick-off, once positioning is agreed. Ads start as soon as the pages can take a lead.

Do you write the copy or do we?

Daily CMO writes it, in your voice, from conversations with you and your customers. You review before anything goes live. Copy that does not sound like the person who shows up makes people feel sold to, so this is not outsourced to a template.

Can you work with our existing website?

Yes. Plenty of clients keep their WordPress site and Daily CMO runs the automations, emails and contacts behind it.

What if it does not work?

Then the offer gets fixed before more money goes into ads. Every step is tracked, so you will know which part is leaking rather than guessing. Reuben will tell you early if he thinks the offer itself is the problem.

Do you run our social media too?

It can be part of the engagement. It is usually the last piece added, once the funnel behind it is converting.
