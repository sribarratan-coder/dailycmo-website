Formats

Three kinds of events, one rule

Whatever the format, the speaker is a practitioner sharing what they actually did, with numbers. No motivational segment, no "high-five the person next to you", no RM4,997 course pitch at the end.

Live webinars

Free, on Zoom, usually an evening or a mid-morning slot. Sixty minutes with a partner expert, then questions. These are the front door to the workshops.

In-person sessions

Half-day talks and sharing sessions in KL, 50 to 100 people, priced low enough to fill the room and high enough that people show up. Ninety-five percent do.

Underdog Con

The full-day marketing conference. 100 to 250 people, 6 to 13 speakers, one theme per edition. What Daily CMO is best known for.

Underdog Con

The marketing conference organised and run by Daily CMO

Case studies. Real insights. Actionable strategies. Speakers are strictly practitioners sharing what they did, what worked and what did not. Deliberately small, because it is not about us. It is about who you sit next to.

No free tickets, ever. Not because we are greedy, but because charging brings the best kind of people together. Everyone goes home with a lesson, a connection, an opportunity, or all three.
